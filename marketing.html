BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few practical answers.

What can I do in the demo?

Create a business project, run the four simulated stages, compare ranked ideas and review business plans. You can open average scores to read all 24 individual agent assessments, inspect existing business details and export project data.

Are the agent scores and revenue real?

No. The agents, scores, reasoning, market metrics, preorders and financial outcomes are simulated. They demonstrate the intended workflow and decision format. Real validation would require external data, actual customer activity and verified order records.

Will my project stay saved?

Projects and stage progress are stored locally in the browser you use. Return in the same browser to continue. Clearing site data removes that local copy, and projects do not sync between devices. The workspace includes an export option.

Can I pause a stage and come back?

Yes. Demo stages support pause and resume. You can also navigate to another business while a stage runs, then return to review its progress and results.

Where can I learn about the investment opportunity?

The investment page presents the product thesis, proposed development priorities and an illustrative funding scenario, with the current prototype clearly distinguished from future capabilities.
